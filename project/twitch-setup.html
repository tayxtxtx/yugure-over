STEP 01 Register Twitch App
  • Go to dev.twitch.tv/console/apps and sign in.
  • Click Register Your Application.
  • Name it anything (e.g. "OBS Overlay"). Set OAuth redirect URL to http://localhost. Category: Broadcasting Suite.
  • Copy your Client ID from the app dashboard and paste it below.
Client Secret is in your Twitch app dashboard — click New Secret if you haven't generated one. It stays in your browser only.
